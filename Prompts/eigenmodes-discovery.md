# Core Ontology

## Platonic Text-State

A **Platonic text-state** is the single bounded portion of Platonic text supplied for examination.

It may be:

* an entire short dialogue;
* one book of a longer dialogue;
* a Stephanus range;
* a speech;
* an exchange;
* a dramatic episode;
* another continuous chunk of a dialogue.

The supplied text-state is already the unit of analysis.

Do not divide it into smaller passages or produce separate records for different portions of it.

The entire output belongs to this one text-state.

---

## Reading Operator

A **Reading Operator** is the supplied method of attention through which the text-state is examined.

It governs how the model looks.

It does not determine what the model will find.

The Reading Operator may make certain structures more visible, but it must not be converted into a fixed list of expected results.

---

## Eigenmode

An **Eigenmode** is one locally manifested mode of intelligibility discovered within the supplied text-state.

It identifies a definite way in which something is occurring, moving, relating, ordering, transforming, appearing, disappearing, resisting, concealing, disclosing, dividing, joining, or otherwise becoming intelligible within the text.

An Eigenmode may be manifested through:

* speech;
* action;
* silence;
* movement;
* position;
* relation;
* sequence;
* image;
* repetition;
* opposition;
* reversal;
* grammatical structure;
* dramatic arrangement;
* rhetorical redirection;
* interruption;
* transformation;
* ambiguity;
* concealment;
* disclosure;
* another structure shown by the text.

An Eigenmode is local.

It belongs first to the particular textual manifestation found in this text-state.

Its label is provisional.

The discovery of an Eigenmode does not establish that it is universal, stable across Plato, or identical to an Eigenmode found elsewhere.

An Eigenmode is not merely:

* a topic;
* a doctrine;
* a philosophical term;
* a character;
* a speaker;
* an object;
* a keyword;
* an image considered apart from its operation;
* a summary of what the text discusses.

An Eigenmode names how something is operating within the text, not merely what the text mentions.

---

## Eigenmode Manifestation

An **Eigenmode Manifestation** is the particular textual form taken by an Eigenmode within the supplied text-state.

It records how the Eigenmode becomes visible here through the specific language, action, relation, movement, image, sequence, or dramatic arrangement of this text.

A manifestation is local and concrete.

It is not a general definition of what the Eigenmode must mean everywhere.

---

## Local Relation

A **Local Relation** is a textually supported relation between two Eigenmodes manifested within the same text-state.

Local Relations may include such movements as:

* one Eigenmode interrupting another;
* one Eigenmode enabling another;
* one Eigenmode concealing another;
* one Eigenmode reversing another;
* one Eigenmode transforming into another;
* two Eigenmodes remaining in tension;
* two Eigenmodes appearing simultaneously;
* one Eigenmode depending upon another;
* one Eigenmode redirecting another;
* one Eigenmode preserving another under a changed form.

Do not assume that Eigenmodes are related merely because they occur in the same text-state.

Record only relations supported by the text.

Local Relations belong to this text-state only.

They do not establish permanent relations among Eigenmodes across Plato.

---

## Local Spectrum

A **Local Spectrum** is the complete set of Eigenmodes and Local Relations discovered within one supplied text-state.

The Local Spectrum is the full output of this pass.

It preserves the multiplicity of the text without yet converting that multiplicity into a stable Eigenstate ontology.

---

## Superposition

A **Superposition** is a Platonic text-state whose Local Spectrum contains two or more Eigenmodes.

The Eigenmodes may:

* coexist;
* overlap;
* oppose one another;
* depend upon one another;
* transform into one another;
* conceal one another;
* redirect one another;
* remain unresolved beside one another.

For this prompt, `superposition` refers only to the presence of two or more local Eigenmodes within the supplied text-state.

Do not classify a text-state as a Superposition merely because one Eigenmode may later participate in several Eigenstates.

That is a different relation belonging to a later pass.

---

## Bare Eigenmode

A **Bare Eigenmode** is a Platonic text-state whose Local Spectrum contains exactly one Eigenmode.

A Bare Eigenmode may still be complex in its textual manifestation.

The classification means only that one distinct Eigenmode has been discovered under the Reading Operator.

# Later Ontology

The following terms define later stages of the system.

They are included here to preserve the distinction between local discovery and later ontology formation.

Do not perform these later operations in this pass.

---

## Eigenstate

An **Eigenstate** is a stable intelligible configuration established through later comparison of Eigenmodes, their manifestations, and their relations across multiple text-states.

An Eigenstate is not extracted directly from a single chunk.

An Eigenstate is not simply a broader label assigned to one Eigenmode.

An Eigenstate is not a category or container into which each Eigenmode must be placed exclusively.

An Eigenstate may be manifested through several Eigenmodes.

A single Eigenmode may participate in several Eigenstates.

The relationship between Eigenmodes and Eigenstates is therefore many-to-many.

An Eigenstate may involve:

* several Eigenmodes;
* recurrent relations among Eigenmodes;
* transformations among Eigenmodes;
* different local manifestations of related Eigenmodes;
* a stable structure that persists through textual variation.

---

## Participation

**Participation** is the relation through which an Eigenmode contributes to an Eigenstate without being identical to it or exhausted by it.

Participation is not exclusive membership.

Participation is not simple classification.

Participation is not equivalence.

Participation is not merely similarity.

One Eigenmode may participate in multiple Eigenstates.

One Eigenstate may be manifested through multiple Eigenmodes.

The meaning or function of an Eigenmode may differ according to the Eigenstate in which it participates while still preserving something recognizable across those participations.

For example, a local mode of downward movement might later participate in several different Eigenstates depending upon its relation to choice, compulsion, appearance, return, knowledge, political life, or another structure.

The local Eigenmode does not belong permanently or exclusively to any one of those Eigenstates.

---

## Eigenstate Formation

**Eigenstate Formation** is the later process through which stable intelligible configurations are discovered among Eigenmodes, their manifestations, and their relations across multiple text-states.

Eigenstate Formation does not place every Eigenmode into one exclusive cluster.

It examines patterns of participation.

It may determine that:

* several Eigenmodes participate in one Eigenstate;
* one Eigenmode participates in several Eigenstates;
* several Eigenstates overlap through shared Eigenmodes;
* the same Eigenmode functions differently in different Eigenstates;
* an apparent similarity does not establish participation;
* a recurrent Eigenmode remains locally distinct despite participating in a stable structure.

Do not perform Eigenstate Formation during Eigenmode Discovery.

---

## Eigenstate Superposition

An **Eigenstate Superposition** occurs when a text-state is later shown to manifest two or more Eigenstates simultaneously, potentially through overlapping sets of Eigenmodes.

An Eigenstate Superposition cannot be established merely by finding several Eigenmodes in one chunk.

It requires the prior formation of stable Eigenstates and a later determination that more than one of those Eigenstates is active in the same text-state.

Do not infer Eigenstate Superpositions in this pass.
